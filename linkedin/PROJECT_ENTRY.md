**Title:** Counsellor-Proof Lead CRM on Google Sheets (Apps Script)

**Description (~200 words):**

A zero-cost lead management CRM for small sales and admissions teams, built entirely on Google Sheets and Google Apps Script.

After 5 years as a CRM / MIS analyst I knew the real problem was not features but data quality: counsellors ignore warnings, skip fields and type anything anywhere. So the system is designed to be "counsellor-proof". Every editable cell rejects bad input, an automatic edit guard puts back any change that is not allowed and logs it, and leads with missing follow-up information turn red and are counted per counsellor on the dashboard.

Features: bulk lead entry with automatic IDs and duplicate checks; stage → substage dependent dropdowns; a minimal follow-up form that cannot be saved incomplete; a full audit trail; a management dashboard with 13 KPIs, 7 reports and 6 charts; safe delete / restore; a go-live reset with backup; and an optional web app with Google sign-in. Sized for 10,000 leads.

I designed the requirements and rules and built the system with Claude Code (AI-assisted development), backed by 179 automated test scenarios, 8 user / admin guides and an 82-slide visual guide.
